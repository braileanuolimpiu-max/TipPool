How it works

Three steps. One shift.

01

Guests tip by QR

One code on every table, or one per person. PayPal or Venmo, in the guest's language. The amount is set on our server, so it can't be changed in the browser.

02

AI splits and explains

Write your rules like you'd say them: "bartenders get 20%, the rest by hours". AI reads them, warns about tip law, explains each share. The money math stays exact, in cents.

03

One payout pays everyone

A close-out check looks for anything odd first. Then one PayPal Payouts batch pays the whole team to PayPal or Venmo, tracked live, never twice.

The agent

Ask it. It does the shift.

Owners ask in plain words and get answers from their own numbers. Ask it to act, and it builds a plan: close out tonight, add the cash, pay everyone. You confirm once; every step is checked again before money moves.

  • The AI can stop a payout, never start one on its own
  • Amounts are calculated in code, never by the AI
  • Every plan is signed, single-use and expires in 10 minutes
