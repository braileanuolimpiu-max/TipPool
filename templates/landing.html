TipPool POS-80

Operator's manual

For restaurants, bars and cafés that split tips as a team.

  1. 01Guests tip by QR

    A QR code on the table or the receipt. Guests pay with PayPal or Venmo, in their own language. No app, no account.

  2. 02The AI splits it, and says why

    Write your house rules in plain words: "bar gets 20%, bussers 10%, servers by hours". TipPool turns them into an exact split, to the cent, with a sentence for every share.

  3. 03One payout pays everyone

    One PayPal batch pays the whole shift. It is checked before it is sent, and the same shift is never paid twice.

  4. 04Ask the agent

    "Close out tonight, add $40 cash and pay everyone." The agent lays out the steps, you confirm once, and it does the shift.

House rules the machine keeps

  • Amounts are calculated in code, never by the AI.
  • The AI can stop a payout. It can never start one.
  • Every split adds up to the cent, and shows its working.
  • Two-step sign-in, a strict security policy and a full history of every change.
